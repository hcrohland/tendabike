# Data Protection

TendaBike is a self-hosted application. Your data stays on your own infrastructure.

## Data stored locally

All data — bikes, parts, activities, service plans — is stored in your local database. No data is sent to any third-party server except Strava (for activity synchronization).

## Strava integration

When you connect your Strava account, TendaBike fetches your activities and bikes via the Strava API. Only the data necessary for maintenance tracking is stored: distance, elevation, date, and bike associations.

## No tracking

TendaBike does not use analytics, cookies, or any tracking mechanisms.

## Your rights

Since TendaBike is self-hosted, you have full control over your data. You can export or delete your data at any time by accessing your database directly.
