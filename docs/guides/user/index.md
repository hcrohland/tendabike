# TendaBike user guide

TendaBike tracks the maintenance of your bikes and parts against how you
actually ride. Connect the service where you log your rides, and the app pulls
in your gear and your riding history. From there it counts your kilometres,
hours, and climbing against the limits you set for each part, so you always
know what's due for service and what isn't.

## Reading order

The topics are in the order a new rider would work through them:

1. [Getting started](onboarding.md) — connect your account and import your
   history.
2. [Your gear](bikes-gear.md) — the bikes the app knows about, and what you can
   do with them.
3. [The components](parts-assemblies.md) — the parts on your bikes: where they
   are, how long they've been there, and how to add, move, or remove them.
4. [Planned maintenance](service-plans.md) — set a service plan, watch the
   badge, and log a service when it's done.
5. [Your rides](activities.md) — the activities the app works from, and the few
   things you can do by hand.
6. [Using a shop](shops.md) — subscribe to a shop, register your gear, and the
   owner's side of it.
7. [Statistics](statistics.md) — your riding as charts: distance, elevation,
   and time over the year.
