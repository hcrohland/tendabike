# Statistics

The **Statistics** page shows your riding as charts: how far you've gone, how
much you've climbed, and how long you've been on the bike, over the course of a
year. It's a way to see your training at a glance — where the volume went up,
which months were big climbers, and how this year stacks against last.

## The controls

At the top of the page, a row of controls narrows what the charts show:

- **Your statistics for** — a year picker. Choose the year the charts are about.
  The options go from the current year back to the oldest year you have rides in.
- **vs** — a second year picker for comparison. Choose another year and its
  numbers are drawn on the same chart as dashed lines, so you can see the two
  years side by side. Leave it on **-- None --** for a single year.
- **Per Month** — a toggle. Off (the default), the charts are cumulative: each
  point is the running total up to that month. On, the charts show one bar per
  month, so you see the month's own contribution rather than the total.
- **Select bikes...** — a multi-select. Pick one or more bikes to limit the
  charts to rides on those bikes. Leave it empty to include all bikes.

## The charts

Three tabs switch between the three charts. Each shows the selected year —
and the comparison year, if you've chosen one.

**Elevation**

The chart is titled **Elevation (m)** and has two lines: **climb** (blue) and
**descend** (green). Each point is the metres climbed and descended. In
cumulative mode the lines climb through the year; in per-month mode each bar is
that month's total climbing and descending.

**Distance**

The chart is titled **Distance (km)** and has one line: the kilometres ridden.
Same cumulative-versus-per-month behaviour as the elevation chart.

**Time**

The chart is titled **Time (h)** and the lines depend on the **Per Month**
toggle:

- **Off** — two lines: **moving time** (blue) and **outdoor time** (green).
  Moving time is the time you were actually riding; outdoor time is the total
  time outdoors, moving or not.
- **On** — two stacked bars per month: **moving time** (blue) on the bottom and
  **pause** (green) on top. The full bar is the total time on the bike that
  month; the green portion is the time you were paused.

## Example

You open **Statistics** in November. The default year is this one. The
**Distance** tab shows a cumulative line that's climbed to about 6,200 km.

You flip **Per Month** on. The line becomes a row of bars, and you can see
September was your biggest month — 1,100 km — while January was quiet at 350.

You pick last year in the **vs** picker. Its distance line comes on the chart as
a dashed blue line, and you can see at a glance that this year you're ahead of
last by about 800 km at this point.

You pick your gravel bike in **Select bikes...**. The charts narrow to gravel
rides only, and the totals drop. You switch to the **Elevation** tab: the climb
line is steeper in per-month mode for the two big Alpine months, and the
comparison year's dashed line shows last year's gravel climbing was lower
overall.
